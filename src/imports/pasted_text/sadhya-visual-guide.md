ASSET SOURCING:

Option 1 — HIGH QUALITY FOOD PHOTOGRAPHY:
- Source high-res food photography from:
  * Professional food photography websites (Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay — free licensed images)
  * Search: "sambar photography", "rasam food photo", etc.
  * Crop to focus on the dish only (remove plate/background)
  * Use PNG with transparent background (via background removal tool)

Option 2 — AI-GENERATED FOOD ILLUSTRATIONS:
- Use AI image generation (DALL-E, Midjourney, Stable Diffusion) to create realistic dish illustrations:
  * Prompt example: "Photorealistic overhead shot of authentic Kerala sambar in a traditional brass bowl, professional food photography, warm lighting, 8k quality"
  * Generate 1 image per dish
  * Export as high-res PNG

Option 3 — COMMISSIONED FOOD ILLUSTRATIONS:
- Hire a food illustrator for realistic Kerala Sadhya dish illustrations
- Higher quality but requires budget + time

RECOMMENDED FOR THIS PROJECT:
Use Option 1 (Photography) or Option 2 (AI-Generated).
Both are fast (1–2 hours total) and look extremely professional.

IMPLEMENTATION IN FIGMA MAKE:

When user draws on banana leaf:
1. Drawn area boundary is captured
2. Selected dish's PHOTOREALISTIC IMAGE fills that area
3. Image is clipped to the drawn shape
4. Slight shadow/border around drawn area for depth

The result: Looks like you're arranging actual food photos on the banana leaf.

VISUAL EFFECT:
- User sees a realistic Sadhya composition
- Each drawn area shows a real-looking food photograph
- Overall impression: "This looks like an actual Sadhya"
- Much more impressive than textures

QUALITY TARGETS:
- Image resolution: 300+ DPI (high quality)
- Aspect ratio: Square or circular (looks best on leaf)
- Background: Transparent (or removed)
- Lighting: Consistent warm/natural Kerala food lighting
- Style: Photorealistic, professional, appetizing
