TEXTURE ANIMATION PRINCIPLES:

Each drawn area should animate to create HYPERREALISTIC effect:

1. LIQUID DISHES (Rasam, Sambar, Curries):
   - Ripple/wave effect across surface
   - Gentle swirling motion
   - Steam wisps rising (semi-transparent)
   - 3-5 second loop, continuous

2. SEMI-THICK DISHES (Avial, Erissery, Olan):
   - Slow settling motion
   - Visible ingredient shifting slightly
   - Gentle ripples (less pronounced)
   - 3-4 second loop

3. DRY/CRISPY DISHES (Pappadam, Upperi, Chips):
   - Minimal animation (mostly static)
   - Subtle shine/reflection shift
   - Slight rotation or flex (pappadam flexing)
   - 4-6 second loop

4. CREAMY DISHES (Payasam, Olan):
   - Gentle swirl/mixing motion
   - Light reflection moving across surface
   - Nuts/raisins appearing to settle
   - 4-5 second loop

5. GRAVY/MEAT DISHES (Curries, Roasts):
   - Liquid ripple + ingredient movement combined
   - Oil sheen shifting
   - Meat pieces settling slightly
   - 3-4 second loop
