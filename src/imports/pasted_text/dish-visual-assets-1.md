OPTION B — BACKGROUND REMOVAL TOOLS:

If you find good food photos online but they have plates:

1. Screenshot or download the image
2. Use FREE background removal tools:
   - remove.bg (online, instant)
   - Cleanup.pictures (AI background removal)
   - Photoshop (Free trial)
   - GIMP (free, open-source)
3. Crop to food only
4. Export as PNG with transparent background
